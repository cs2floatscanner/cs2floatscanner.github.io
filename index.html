From finding a float
to sorting a collection.

The market tools you know, with a new workspace for the items you own.

Know what you’re looking at.

Float, Paint Seed and rare-pattern tiers help you compare listings. Filter the page and copy exact values without opening each item in the game.

  • Support for Steam’s new grouped market pages
  • Native float and seed details stay in one place
  • Built-in and custom pattern tiers

Move a group.
Keep the collection in order.

Open your inventory and Storage Units side by side. Select cases, skins or stickers together and choose where they go.

  • QR sign-in confirmed through Steam Guard
  • Deposit and withdraw, with a confirmation before starting
  • Search Storage Units by name or ID and check free capacity

Choose the float range.
Select the matching items.

Find low-float skins, pick the first N items or select a range with Shift. Group by type, name or wear and sort each list independently.

A filter can hide items without clearing your selection. The workspace shows when selected items are outside the current filter.

The item image,
ready to share.

Export a PNG with float, seed and the available price. Supported Steam listings use their existing gallery image, matched to the exact listing.

Copy the card or download it. If an image cannot be loaded, you get a retry option.

See how much you’ve priced.

Inventory, Storage Unit and account totals use the prices you have already loaded. Loading another item type updates the sums automatically.

Priced 100 / 200 means only those 100 items are included. The account total includes your inventory and verified contents of Storage Units you have opened. Filters and selection do not change the totals.

Charts and estimates

Technical indicators, price scenarios, buy-order estimates and Steam fee calculations. A basis for comparison, not a promise of returns.

Steam prices in Storage

Load a price beside an item to value every matching copy. See totals before fees and approximate proceeds after fees, with a count of priced items. Quotes are cached by currency; stale prices are marked after six hours.

Alerts and item details

Float alerts, sticker wear, charm patterns and configurable highlights keep relevant information close to the listing.

A workspace that fits

Dark, light or automatic theme, compact rows, image and ID controls. Scanner supports ten languages; Storage supports English and Russian.

Start on the market.
Connect Storage when you need it.

Market scanning and Storage sign-in are separate. You can browse supported Steam pages without connecting the Storage workspace.

For Steam Market

  1. Install from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open a CS2 listing or your Steam inventory.
  3. Use float filters, pattern tiers and the item tools.

For Storage in 1.9.0

  1. Close CS2 and open Storage from the extension.
  2. Confirm the QR code with Steam Guard.
  3. Choose a Storage Unit, select items and review the transfer.

Keep the management tab open during transfers. A Storage Unit must already be in your inventory.

Direct to Valve.
Clear about the data.

Storage authentication and item commands connect directly to Valve. There is no developer authentication backend.

Read the privacy policy
  • The token stays in the tabNot stored in Chrome Storage or synced. Reloading or closing the management tab clears the local session.
  • Settings stay localView preferences and public price caches are saved on your device.
  • Network access is part of the workflowValve services, Steam images, optional public price sources and the extension-user-count check make external requests.
