The tools, without the clutter.

Open a topic for the details.

Storage connects directly to Valve. Optional USD pricing downloads a public CSFloat feed; account data is not sent to that provider. Read the privacy policy

Market and rare patterns

Float, Seed, sticker wear, charm patterns, rare tiers and alerts. Sort, filter and copy exact values. New and classic Steam Market support, including SIH replacement cards. Export a PNG with the exact listing’s available Steam image.

Storage and bulk transfers

Steam Guard QR sign-in, bulk selection and confirmed transfers. Move items into, out of or between Storage Units. Rename a unit, or buy one through Steam with a separate payment confirmation and assign its name. Items listed for sale are marked and blocked from moves and crafting.

Account search and USD valuation

Search your inventory and all verified loaded Storage Units by name, float, pattern or ID. Reuse a shared CSFloat USD price list to estimate a unit, account or selection. See priced-item coverage and approximate proceeds after fees. Unread contents and missing prices stay unknown.

Trade-ups and profit search

Build 10-skin or 5-Covert contracts from eligible items of matching rarity and StatTrak mode. Compare outcome odds, predicted float and wear, cost, expected value, ROI and profit chance after fees. Search owned items for the best found combinations. Craft only after a separate consumption confirmation; estimates never guarantee profit.

Charts and market analysis

Price history, technical indicators, price scenarios and buy-order estimates. Keep Steam Market analysis separate from CSFloat USD listing estimates. Quotes exclude float, sticker and pattern premiums; prices and outcomes are not guaranteed.

Your display and workflow

Themes, float precision, compact rows, item images and filters. Streamer mode hides account identity, IDs and amounts while Storage Unit names stay visible. Scanner has ten languages; Storage and crafting use English or Russian.

How to start

Install from the Chrome Web Store and open a CS2 listing. For Storage, close CS2, open Storage in the extension and confirm the QR code with Steam Guard.
