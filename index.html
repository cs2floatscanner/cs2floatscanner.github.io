The tools, without the clutter.

Open a topic for the details.

Storage connects directly to Valve. The sign-in token stays in the management tab. Read the privacy policy

Market and rare patterns

Float, Seed, sticker wear and charm patterns, built-in or custom rare tiers, and float alerts. Filter, sort and copy exact values. Export a PNG with the matching Steam image: copy, download or retry if an image fails to load.

Storage and bulk transfers

Steam Guard QR sign-in. Select by group, Shift, quantity or float; review deposits and withdrawals before they start.

Charts and price totals

Price history, technical indicators, price scenarios and buy-order estimates. Inventory and Storage totals before and after estimated fees use known prices only. Account totals include verified opened Storage contents; estimates do not guarantee returns.

Your display and workflow

Themes, float precision, compact rows, item images and ID search. Works alongside CSFloat Market Checker and Steam Inventory Helper. Scanner has ten languages; Storage has English and Russian.

How to start

Install from the Chrome Web Store and open a CS2 listing. For Storage, close CS2, open Storage in the extension and confirm the QR code with Steam Guard.
